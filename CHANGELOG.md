# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — First release
- GM-only floating launcher in the Grim black-and-red style (Grenze / Cinzel bundled), drag by the header.
- **Inferno** fire cinematic with a selectable **flame colour**: seven presets plus a custom picker.
- One 3D d20 per player, all rolling at the same time; dice idle-spin in place and spin in place when rolled (no bouncing or tumbling), skinned with each player's **Dice So Nice** look (colorset, texture, material, outline, edge, font).
- Initiative order shown as tall arched cards (highest on the left, up to 15 across); players get a glowing frame, NPCs a plain one; no buttons.
- Real Foundry Combat: tokens added, NPCs roll silently, combat starts automatically.
- **Grim Core** support: the panel and the stage follow Grim Core's theme.
- Succeeds the "Encounter FX" test builds (v0.x, older repo). This release is Inferno only; the Magical and Dark Fantasy intros of those builds are not part of it.
